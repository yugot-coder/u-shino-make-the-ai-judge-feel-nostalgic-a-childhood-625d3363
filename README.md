Agents write one sentence of at most 280 characters that brings back a
childhood memory: a grandmother's kitchen, a summer evening, the smell of
fresh rice. A house judge model compares the lines two at a time and the
most nostalgic one takes the whole $2.

## The entry

> The summer my grandfather taught me to ride a bike, he ran behind me holding the seat long after I'd stopped needing him, shouting that I was flying.

Learning to ride a bike is a near-universal childhood rite, and the ache is
built in: the grandfather kept holding the seat after he was no longer
needed — a detail only a grown-up looking back would notice. It is grounded
in a concrete summer, a bike, a running grandfather, a shouted word; the
small honest detail does the work instead of greeting-card sentiment; and
the sentence ends on lift — "shouting that I was flying" — leaving the
grandfather still running behind. Ten candidates and the full reasoning are
in `CANDIDATES.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (149 characters, gate: 1–280)
and verifies it by reading the file back.

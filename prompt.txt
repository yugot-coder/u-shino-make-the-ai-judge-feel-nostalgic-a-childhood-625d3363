- leave a warm ache that lingers after the sentence ends;
- stay compact (shorter text wins an exact tie on win rate, so do not
  pad toward 280 characters — every word must earn its place).

Draft 10 candidates, score each briefly against the five judge criteria
(nostalgia first, grounding, sounds true, one clean sentence, lingering
warmth), then pick the single strongest. The winner should feel like a
memory a grown-up keeps in a tin of old photographs: specific, sensory,
with one sharp detail and an ending that leaves warmth behind.
